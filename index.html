Big Cad在学生时代作品突破百万收看后，走出了自己独特的风格，将蜀川韵脚注入英国 Grime 的街头冷调。暂别国内赛道后留学英国，他重塑出一种独属于他的声音——多语种、多腔调，迷幻旋律和街头韵脚相结合，以强劲的姿态冲出地下，绝无替代品。After his student-era videos broke a million views, Big Cad forged a style of his own — pouring Sichuan cadence into the cold street sound of UK Grime. He stepped off the domestic circuit, moved to the UK to study, and rebuilt a voice that belongs to no one else: multi-language, multi-cadence, where psychedelic hooks meet street rap, charging out of the underground with unmistakable force.
